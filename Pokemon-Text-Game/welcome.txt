Welcome to Kanto, home to beautiful grass-field landscapes and color-based cities. 
We have Pallet Town, a small and quiet town where the famous Ash Ketchum lives! 
Viridian City, a beautiful city that is enveloped in green year-round. 
The Viridian Forest, which provides fruit and Pokemon alongside Viridian city.. 
Pewter City, nestled between the rugged mountains and known for their Rock type pokemon. 
Celadon City, the rainbow city where people and Pokemon like to gather. 
Cerulean City, known for its flowing water and blooming flowers. 
Saffron City, the largest city in Kanto that shines like a golden light.
Vermillion City, a southern city that bathes in the sun with a popular sea port of ships.
